# Northwind Retail — Returns Policy

Customers may return unused items in original packaging within 30 days of
delivery for a full refund. Items marked final sale are not eligible.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the
returned item arriving at the warehouse. Delivery charges are refunded only when
the return is due to a fault or a picking error on our side.

For faulty goods the 30-day window does not apply; statutory rights continue for
six months from delivery.

Store staff can process an in-store return for an online order provided the
customer presents the order confirmation email.
