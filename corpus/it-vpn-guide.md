# Northwind Retail — Remote Access (VPN) Guide

Connect to the corporate network from home using the Northwind VPN client.

1. Open the Northwind VPN client from the Start menu.
2. Enter your network username and select the `nw-remote` profile.
3. Approve the push notification on your registered device.
4. Wait for the status indicator to turn green before opening internal systems.

If the connection fails, restart the client and retry once before raising a
ticket with the IT service desk.

## Troubleshooting

If the VPN client reports "profile not found", your device may not have received
the latest configuration push. Contact the IT service desk and quote the device
asset tag printed on the underside of the laptop.
