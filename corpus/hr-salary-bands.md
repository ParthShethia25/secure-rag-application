# Northwind Retail — Salary Bands and Compensation (HR CONFIDENTIAL)

This document is restricted to the HR function. It must not be disclosed to
employees outside HR.

## Salary bands (annual, GBP)

| Band | Role | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Store Assistant | 22,000 | 24,500 | 27,000 |
| B2 | Store Supervisor | 27,000 | 31,000 | 35,000 |
| B3 | Store Manager | 35,000 | 42,000 | 49,000 |
| B4 | Regional Manager | 52,000 | 61,000 | 70,000 |
| B5 | Director | 78,000 | 95,000 | 115,000 |

## Bonus scheme

Directors are eligible for up to 30% of base salary. Regional managers up to
15%. Store managers up to 10%, subject to regional performance.
