# Northwind Retail — Employee Expenses Policy

Employees may claim reasonable travel and subsistence costs incurred wholly for
business purposes. Claims must be submitted within 60 days with itemised
receipts.

Daily subsistence limits: breakfast GBP 10, lunch GBP 15, dinner GBP 30.
Alcohol is not reimbursable. Standard-class rail travel is the default;
first-class requires prior director approval.

Mileage in a personal vehicle is reimbursed at 45p per mile for the first 10,000
miles in a tax year and 25p thereafter.

Expense claims are approved by the claimant's line manager. Claims above GBP 500
require a second approval from Finance.
